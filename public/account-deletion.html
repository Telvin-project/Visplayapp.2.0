Delete your VisPlay account

You can request permanent deletion of your VisPlay account and associated personal data from the VisPlay website. You do not need to reinstall the mobile app to start this process.

  1. Open VisPlay and sign in to the account you want to delete.
  2. Open Library, then select Account Profile.
  3. Scroll to Delete Account, read the deletion notice, and select Permanently Delete My Account.
  4. Confirm only if you want the deletion to proceed. The account and eligible associated data will be removed.

Deletion removes your profile, comments, likes, follows, viewing history, notifications and creator uploads associated with the account. If you are a creator, your uploaded movies and related creator content may be removed from VisPlay.

Records that must be retained for accounting, payment disputes, security or legal compliance may be kept in anonymized form. Any remaining wallet balance will be lost when the account is deleted. Review the confirmation notice before proceeding.
